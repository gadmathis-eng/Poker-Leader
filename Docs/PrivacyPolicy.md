# Pot Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Pot Master ("we", "us", or "our") is a home poker tracking app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

If you have questions, contact us at **support@potmaster.app**.

## Information We Collect

### Account information

When you sign in (optional), we receive an account identifier from Supabase Auth. Depending on how you sign in, this may include:

- Email address (email one-time passcode sign-in)
- Name or email from Apple (Sign in with Apple)
- Name or email from Google (Sign in with Google)

We also store the display name and player handle you choose in the app.

### App data you create

To provide circle and session features, we store data you enter, including:

- Circle names, invite codes, members, and default buy-in or currency settings
- Session titles, buy-ins, final stacks, settlement payments, and timestamps
- Friend request metadata (sender, recipient handle, status)
- Leaderboard and history derived from the above

### Information stored only on your device

If you do not sign in, or before data syncs, some information may exist only locally on your iPhone using SwiftData. Local data stays on the device unless you enable cloud sync by signing in.

### Information we do not collect

We do not collect:

- Payment card or bank account details
- Precise location
- Contacts from your address book
- Advertising identifiers
- Health or biometric data

We do not use the app for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## How We Use Information

We use your information only to operate Pot Master, including to:

- Authenticate you and sync your circles and sessions across devices
- Let you join circles and share game data with other members of those circles
- Calculate settlements, history, and leaderboards
- Send or display in-app notifications related to friend requests or circle activity
- Respond to support requests and legal obligations
- Maintain security and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal information.

## Sharing With Other Users

When you join or create a circle, other members of that circle can see relevant game data for that circle, such as display names, handles, buy-ins, final stacks, and settlement results. Do not join circles or enter information you do not want shared with that group.

## Third-Party Services

Pot Master uses third-party services to provide core functionality:

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Supabase** | Authentication, database, and cloud sync |
| **Apple** | Sign in with Apple (if you choose it) |
| **Google** | Sign in with Google (if you choose it) |

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing:

- [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We do not control how those services handle data outside Pot Master.

## Data Retention

We keep your account and synced data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your cloud profile and associated synced data, subject to reasonable backup retention by our infrastructure providers.

If you use the app only locally without signing in, deleting the app from your device removes local data on that device.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures through Supabase and Apple platform security to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices and Rights

You can:

- Use the app locally without signing in
- Update your display name and handle in Settings
- Sign out at any time
- Delete your account in the app (see below)

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact **support@potmaster.app** and we will respond within a reasonable time.

## Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app:

1. Open **You**
2. Tap the gear icon to open **Settings**
3. Scroll to **Account**
4. Tap **Delete account**

This permanently deletes your Pot Master account, cloud profile, circles you own, and synced data associated with your account on the device.

If you cannot access the app, email **support@potmaster.app** from the address tied to your account and we will delete your account within 30 days.

## Children's Privacy

Pot Master is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact **support@potmaster.app** and we will delete it.

## International Users

If you use Pot Master outside your home country, your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate, including the United States and the European Union. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Pot Master after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

Questions or privacy requests: **support@potmaster.app**
